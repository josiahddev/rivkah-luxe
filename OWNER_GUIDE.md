## 1. Logging in

1. Go to **yourwebsite.com/admin** (for example `www.rivkahluxe.com/admin`).
2. Type your **email** and **password**, then press **Sign in**.
3. Forgot your password? Press **Forgot password?**, type your email, and we'll send you a link to choose a new one (the link works for 1 hour).

`[Screenshot: sign-in page]`

**Using your phone:** the dashboard works on any phone or tablet. Tap the **☰** button (top left) to open the menu. Add the page to your home screen for one-tap access:
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*.

**Always sign out** on shared computers: menu → your name → **Sign out**.

## 2. Your dashboard at a glance

The **Home** page shows:
- **Today's sales**, **This week**, **This month** and **All-time revenue** — money from paid orders, in Canadian dollars.
- **Orders to pack** — paid orders waiting for you.
- **Low on stock** — items that need restocking.
- **Sales, last 30 days** chart, **Best sellers**, **Latest orders** and **Running low**.

The first time you sign in you'll also see **"Let's set up your store"** — a 7-step checklist. Each step ticks itself off when it's done. Press **Hide this guide** when you no longer need it.

The **🔔 bell** (top right) shows notifications: new orders, payments, failed payments, low stock, new customers, new reviews and messages.

## 3. Adding a product

1. Press **+ Add product** (top of every page).
2. **Product details:** type the **Product name**, a **Short description** (one or two sentences) and the **Full description**. Use the buttons above the text box for **bold**, lists and headings.
3. **Photos:** drag your photos into the box (see [Uploading photos](#4-uploading-photos)).
4. **Price & stock:**
   - **Price** — what customers pay, in Canadian dollars (e.g. `32`).
   - **Sale price** *(optional)* — a lower price to show a sale.
   - **Cost to you** *(optional, private)* — never shown to customers.
   - **How many in stock** and **Warn me when stock reaches** (e.g. `5`).
5. **Different sizes or scents?** Press **Add sizes or scents**. Choose what they differ by (Size, Volume, Fragrance…), then give each option a name (e.g. `237ml`), price and stock. Press **Add another option** for more.
6. **Ingredients & how to use:** copy these **from your label only**. Avoid medical claims like "cures" or "treats".
7. On the right: choose **Live in shop** (or **Draft** to finish later), the **Category**, and tick **Feature on homepage** if you want it in "The Signatures".
8. Press **Add product** at the bottom.

**Other handy buttons on a product page:**
- **Duplicate** — copy a product (great for a new scent). The copy starts as a Draft with 0 stock.
- **View in shop** — see it as customers do.
- **Delete** — removes it for good. Prefer setting it to **Archived** if you may sell it again.

## 4. Uploading photos

1. Open the product (**Products** → click it).
2. In **Photos**, drag photos from your computer into the box — or tap the box on your phone to choose from your camera roll.
3. The **first photo is the main one**. Use **← →** under each photo to change the order, and the **×** to remove one.
4. Type a short description under each photo (e.g. "Peach Bloom body oil bottle") — it helps blind visitors and Google.
5. Press **Save product**.

Big photos are resized automatically — you don't need to edit them first. **Tips for beautiful photos:** natural daylight, a plain cream or black background, the label facing the camera, and the same angle for every product.

All your uploads are also in **Photos** (menu → Website → Photos), where you can reuse them anywhere and see where each one is used.

## 5. Changing a price (and running a sale)

1. **Products** → click the product.
2. Under **Price & stock**, type the new **Price**.
3. For a sale, type a lower number in **Sale price** — customers see the old price crossed out. Empty the box to end the sale.
4. Press **Save product**. The change is live straight away.

Every price change is recorded in the **Activity log**, e.g. *"Rivkah changed Peach Bloom (189ml) price from $26 to $27."*

## 6. Updating stock

**The quick way:** menu → **Stock**.
- Find the item (use **Search**, or **Low or out** to see what needs attention).
- Choose **Set to** and type the new number — *or* choose **Add (restock)** and type how many just arrived.
- Press **Update**.

Stock **goes down automatically** when an order is paid, and **goes back up** if you cancel or refund an order. When an item reaches 0 it shows **"Out of stock"** in the shop and can't be bought.

**Many products at once:** **Products** → tick the boxes → choose **Set stock to…** or **Add stock (restock)…** → **Apply**.

## 7. Creating a discount code

1. Menu → **Discount codes** → **+ Create code**.
2. Type the **code** customers will enter (e.g. `GLOW15`).
3. Choose **Percentage off**, **Amount off** or **Free shipping**, and the amount.
4. *(Optional rules)* start/end dates, minimum order, total number of uses, uses per customer, **first order only**, or only certain products/categories.
5. Press **Create code**, then share it (Instagram, thank-you cards, WhatsApp…).

Use the **on/off switch** in the list to pause a code without deleting it. The **Used** column shows how many times it's been used.

## 8. Processing an order

When a customer pays, you'll see a 🔔 notification (and an email, if set up in Settings).

1. Menu → **Orders** → **To pack**.
2. Click the order. You'll see the **items**, the **customer** (email, phone, a **WhatsApp** button), the **shipping address** and any **customer note** (gift messages appear here).
3. Pack the order, then under **Update progress** press **Processing** (or **Ready for Delivery**) → **Update**.
4. Keep **"Email the customer about this update"** ticked — they get a beautiful email each time.

**Print an invoice/packing slip:** press **Print invoice** (top right) → print, or choose *Save as PDF*.

**Internal notes** (only your team sees them) are at the bottom of the order — handy for "Customer asked for gift wrap".

## 9. Marking an order as shipped

1. Open the order.
2. Press **Shipped**.
3. In **Message for the customer**, paste the **tracking number or link** (e.g. Canada Post).
4. Press **Update to Shipped**. The customer is emailed and can follow it on the **Track your order** page.
5. Later, mark it **Out for Delivery** and **Delivered** the same way.

## 10. Cancelling or refunding an order

1. **First refund the money** in your **Stripe** dashboard (card payments) or **Paystack** dashboard (naira payments): find the payment → **Refund**.
2. Then open the order in your dashboard and press **Refunded** (or **Cancelled** if it was never paid).
3. Confirm. The items are **added back to your stock** automatically and the customer is emailed.

## 11. Adding a new category

1. Menu → **Categories**.
2. In **Add a category** (right side): type the name (e.g. *Hair Oils*), a short description, and add a photo.
3. Tick **Visible in the shop** and **Show on the homepage**.
4. Press **Add category**.
5. Put products in it from each product's page (**Organise → Category**), or tick several products in **Products** → **Change category**.

**Collections** (on the same page) are curated edits like *New Arrivals* or *Christmas Gifts*. A product can be in several collections.

## 12. Changing the homepage banner

1. Menu → **Website content** → **Homepage**.
2. Each part of the homepage is listed from top to bottom:
   - **Announcement bar** — the thin strip at the very top (one message per line).
   - **1. Hero** — the big photo, headline and buttons.
   - **5. Promotional banner**, **7. Brand story**, **9. Instagram photos**, and more.
3. To change a photo, press **Change** on it and drag in a new one.
4. Edit the words, then press **Save section**. It's live immediately — press **View homepage** to check.
5. Untick **Show this section** to hide a section.

For seasonal campaigns that start and end by themselves, use [Promotions](#18-promotions).

## 13. Changing your contact information

1. Menu → **Settings** → **Store & contact**.
2. Update your **business email**, **phone**, **WhatsApp number** (with country code, e.g. `+1 416 555 0100`), **address** and **opening hours**.
3. Press **Save**. Your footer, contact page, invoices and emails update everywhere.

Social links (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook…) are under **Settings → Social & WhatsApp**. Adding a WhatsApp number shows **"Chat with us on WhatsApp"** buttons across your site.

## 14. Setting shipping fees

1. Menu → **Shipping**.
2. Each **zone** has a fee, an optional **"free when order is over"** amount and a **delivery time** shown to customers. The demo zones are Canada, United States, Nigeria and Rest of the world.
3. Press **Edit** to change one, or fill in **Add a shipping zone**.
4. You can make a zone for **just some provinces/states** (e.g. *Ontario* with code `ON`) — it wins over the whole-country zone.
5. Keep one zone ticked as **Everywhere else** so every customer can check out.

## 15. Currencies and sales tax

**Currencies** (Settings → **Currencies**): you set prices in **Canadian dollars**. Customers can switch to **USD** or **NGN** at the top of the page (Nigerian visitors see naira automatically). Type today's exchange rate (e.g. 1 CAD = 1100 NGN) and **update it every so often** — rates don't change by themselves.

**Sales tax** (Settings → **Tax**): when on, Canadian orders are charged the GST/HST/PST rate for the delivery province. The rates are pre-filled — **please confirm them with your accountant**. If your sales are under $30,000 a year you may be a "small supplier" and not need to charge tax; untick **Charge sales tax** if so.

## 16. Reading your sales reports

Menu → **Sales reports**. Choose **Last 7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **12 months** at the top.

| Box | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Revenue** | Money from paid orders (cancelled/refunded excluded) |
| **Average order** | Revenue ÷ number of orders |
| **Checkout conversion** | Of the bags people started, how many became paid orders |
| **Repeat customers** | Buyers who've ordered more than once |

Below: **Revenue by day**, **Orders by day**, **New customer accounts**, **Best-selling products**, **Sales by category**, and **Abandoned bags** (people who added items but didn't pay). Press **Show as table** under a chart to see the exact numbers. Only email abandoned-bag customers marked **"opted in"** — Canada's anti-spam law requires consent.

## 17. Approving reviews

New reviews are **hidden until you approve them**.

1. Menu → **Reviews** → **Waiting for approval**.
2. For each review press **Approve** (publish), **Hide** (keep private), or **★ Feature on homepage**.
3. Reviews marked **Verified purchase** were written by someone who bought that product.

You can add testimonials you received by message with **Add a testimonial** — only share real reviews you have permission to use.

## 18. Promotions

Menu → **Promotions** → **+ New promotion**.

1. Name it (e.g. *Christmas 2026*), write the **headline**, text and button, and add a photo.
2. Optionally connect a **collection** (where the button goes) and a **discount code** to mention.
3. Choose **Replace the big homepage hero** and/or **Show as the homepage banner**.
4. Set **Starts** and **Ends** — it appears and disappears by itself.
5. Press **Create promotion**.

---

## 19. Messages and email subscribers

- **Messages** (menu → Website → Messages): enquiries from your Contact page. Press **Reply by email** (or WhatsApp), then **Mark resolved**.
- **Email subscribers** (menu → Grow → Email subscribers): everyone who signed up for your newsletter. Press **Export CSV** to download the list for Mailchimp, Klaviyo, etc.

## 20. Adding team members

Menu → **Team** → **Add a team member**. Choose their access:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner (full access)** | Everything, including settings, payments and the team |
| **Store Manager** | Products, stock, orders, customers, discounts, shipping, reports |
| **Content Manager** | Website content, banners, promotions, reviews, photos |

Give them a temporary password privately; they can change it with "Forgot password". Remove access any time from the same page. Everything people change is listed in the **Activity log**.

## 21. Before you launch — checklist

- [ ] Add your real products, photos, prices and stock.
- [ ] **Delete the demo products** (marked "Demo") — ask your developer to run the remove-demo command to clear demo orders and customers too.
- [ ] Replace the homepage photos and words (Website content → Homepage).
- [ ] Check every page: About, Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms, Cookies (Website content → Pages). Have the legal pages reviewed.
- [ ] Set your shipping fees and delivery times.
- [ ] Check currencies/exchange rates and tax.
- [ ] Add your contact details, WhatsApp and social links.
- [ ] Payments connected (Settings → Payments shows **Connected · Live**).
- [ ] Place one real small order yourself, then refund it.
- [ ] **Settings → Publish → Store is open to customers** → Save. 🎉

## 22. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A customer says they paid but the order shows "Awaiting payment" | Check the payment in Stripe/Paystack. If it's paid there, the order will usually update within minutes. If not, contact your developer. |
| "⚠ Check" on an order | Open it — the red box explains (e.g. stock ran out as payment arrived). Contact the customer, then press **Mark as resolved**. |
| A photo won't upload | Use JPG or PNG under 10 MB. Try again on Wi-Fi. |
| I can't find a page in the menu | Your team role may not include it — ask the store owner. |
| Forgot password | Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. |

Your developer has the technical guide (README.md) with everything about hosting, payments, email and backups.
